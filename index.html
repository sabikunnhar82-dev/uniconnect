UniConnect

One login for students at Southern Coast University, instead of the four separate portals they put up with now. SCU is a made-up university for Scenario C of the brief. This is our group's project site for BISY2004 Assessment 2: the plan, the tool evidence, and where the project stands.

Problem and purpose

Right now students have to use four different portals, each with its own login. Records get duplicated and the privacy controls aren't the same across them. 38% of the 4,200 helpdesk tickets each month are portal or password problems. UniConnect gives students one portal with single sign-on and MFA and clean migrated data, and it meets the Privacy Act and WCAG 2.1 AA (Privacy Act 1988 (Cth)) (W3C, 2018). It also can't go down during enrolment.

Methodology: hybrid

We went hybrid. The semester deadline is fixed and privacy and data migration are risky, so that side needs proper gates, baselines and change control. Portal features change as students give feedback, so we build those in two-week sprints and track them in Trello (PMI & Agile Alliance, 2017).

  1. Initiate, ends at M1 Charter approved (predictive)
  2. Plan & Design, ends at M2 Design sign-off (predictive)
  3. Build (Sprints 1–4), ends at M3 Build complete (agile)
  4. Migrate & Test, ends at M4 Trial migration passed / M5 UAT sign-off (predictive)
  5. Deploy & Transition, ends at M6 Go-live (predictive)
  6. Close, ends at M7 Project closed (predictive)

SMART objectives

#Objective
O1Launch one portal with SSO + MFA for all 24,000 students by 20 Jan 2027 (no later than 5 Feb 2027).
O2Migrate student records from four portals with ≥ 99.5% reconciliation accuracy by the M4 gate.
O3Pass the WCAG 2.1 AA audit with 0 critical failures before the M5 UAT sign-off.
O4Keep portal p95 page load under 2 s at 5,000 concurrent users during the Feb 2027 enrolment peak.
O5Cut portal-related helpdesk tickets by 30% within three months of go-live (by 30 Apr 2027).
O6Deliver within the approved budget of BAC + contingency reserve.
