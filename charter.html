Interpretation

Four stakeholders could stop the project: the sponsor (money), the CIO (architecture), the Registrar (owns the data) and the Privacy Officer (can halt migration). We manage them closely and they sit on the steering committee or the CCB. The Deans have a lot of power but aren't keen, so their feature requests go through change control. Students care a lot but have no formal power, so the SRC speaks for them in sprint reviews (PMI, 2021).
