R01: from risk to issue

R01 (bad legacy data) scored 16: likely, with a big schedule hit on the critical path. Our early profiling only checked 5% of the records, so the mitigation only half worked and the trigger went off in trial migration 1. R01 turned into ISS-01, and its $39,000 of contingency now pays for CR-02. Once the cleansing rules and a second trial are in place, R01 drops to 6 (PMI, 2021).

What we decided

After CR-02, keep the rest of the $90,750 contingency for R04 (cutover clash) and R07 (load), and don't release it until the load test passes.
